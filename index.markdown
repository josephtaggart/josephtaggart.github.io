## About me

I am a Research Assistant at the <a href="https://warwick.ac.uk/fac/sci/statistics/staff/academic-research/taggart/">University of Warwick</a> and <a href="https://magentaresearch.org/team/joe-taggart/">Swansea University</a>. My research at Warwick focuses on Bayesian predictive modelling of Patient Reported Outcome Measures (PROMs), particularly those concerning quality of life, using routinely collected healthcare data. At Swansea, I am part of the MAGENTA research project, where I am looking at the association between exposure to extreme temperatures and maternity outcomes.

## Research interests
I am particularly interested in prediction modelling and observational data, and the statistical challenges surrounding them.
